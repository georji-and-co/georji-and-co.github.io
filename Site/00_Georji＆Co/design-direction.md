# Georji & Co Design Direction

## Core Mood
- Quiet luxury
- Editorial feeling
- Emotional but minimal
- Spacious layouts
- Cinematic atmosphere

## Avoid
- Template feeling
- Cheap gradients
- Aggressive CTA
- Over-animation
- SaaS-style UI
- Loud marketing language

## Typography
- Typography-first layouts
- Elegant spacing
- Strong heading hierarchy
- Minimal text density

## Layout
- Wide spacing
- Strong visual rhythm
- Photo-driven sections
- Breathing room between blocks

## UI Direction
- Calm interaction
- Refined hover effects
- Minimal borders
- Natural shadows
- Subtle motion only

## Brand References
- Patagonia
- Aesop
- Apple
- Kinfolk
- Arc'teryx
